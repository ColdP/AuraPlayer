## 2. Local Files and Audio Processing Risks

2.1 Tag and Artwork Editing: Editing song titles, artists, albums, lyrics, or embedded artwork writes directly to the original audio files on your device. Although the app handles common formats carefully, corrupted source files, non-standard encodings, concurrent edits by other apps, or insufficient storage may cause write failures or file corruption. Always keep backups of important media files.

2.2 Audio Gain and Hearing Safety: Custom preamp gain, EQ boosts, dynamic compression, and high output volume may cause audio clipping, speaker or headphone overload, or hearing damage over prolonged listening. Keep automatic gain protection enabled when appropriate and listen at safe volume levels.
