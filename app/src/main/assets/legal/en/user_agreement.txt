Aura Player User Agreement

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last revised: November 14, 2026

Welcome to Aura Player ("the software"), an open-source local and NetEase Cloud Music player for Android 13 and above built with MIUIX and Media3, offering Hyper, Material, and One interface styles. Before using the software, please read this Agreement, the Disclaimer, and the Privacy Policy to understand feature boundaries, copyright obligations, and how data is handled.

## 1. Acceptance and Usage

1.1 Installing, opening, or using the software indicates that you have read and understood this Agreement, the Disclaimer, and the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to these terms, please stop using and uninstall the software.

1.2 The software supports both pure local playback and optional online music services. You can use it offline as a local music player without signing in to any account; online playback, playlist syncing, artwork fetching, and lyric matching are triggered only when you use those features.

## 2. Environment and Core Features

2.1 The software is built and tested for Android 13 (API 33) and above. OEM system customizations, audio drivers, or background restrictions on specific devices may affect visual effects or background playback behavior.

2.2 Core features include local library scanning and management, built-in audio effects and equalizer presets (with JSON and QR import/export), tag and cover editing, lyric sharing, and optional NetEase Cloud Music login, streaming, search, and downloads.

## 3. Third-Party Music Services and Copyright Compliance

3.1 Only play, search, share, or download music content that you are legally authorized to access. NetEase Cloud Music and other external music or lyric services are operated by their respective providers, and their account, regional, VIP, and purchase restrictions remain fully applicable.

3.2 Music downloads use valid download URLs returned by NetEase Cloud Music together with the Android system downloader, saving tracks to public music storage (such as Music/Aura Player). This software does not bypass paywalls, unlock paid content, or circumvent DRM.
